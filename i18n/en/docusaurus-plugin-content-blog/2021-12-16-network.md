Because communication facilities are being relocated for river construction work in the prefecture,
the network will be temporarily unavailable during the following time period.

<!-- truncate -->

## Date and time

Thursday, 16 December 2021, 04:00 to 06:00 (24-hour clock)
  - A communication outage of about 60 minutes will occur during this period.


## Scope of impact
  - While the network is down, you cannot log in to the supercomputer or transfer data.
  - Running jobs are not stopped.


We ask for your understanding and cooperation.
